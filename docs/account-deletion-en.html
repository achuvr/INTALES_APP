Account & Associated Data Deletion

This page is for users who wish to delete their account for the mobile application "INTALES (intalescafe)" and the data associated with that account. You can request deletion using either of the methods below.

Operator: Masanori Akiyama (秋山真範), trade name "intalescafe"  /  Contact: intaleslab@gmail.com

Option 1: Delete from within the app (recommended, immediate)

If you can still use the app, you can delete your account yourself, immediately, from within the app.

  1. Launch the app and log in.
  2. Open the Information screen from the home screen.
  3. Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the screen.
  4. On the confirmation dialogs, tap "Proceed with deletion" and then "Delete permanently" to execute the deletion.

This action cannot be undone. Once deletion is complete, you are automatically logged out and returned to the start screen.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (e.g., if you have uninstalled the app)

If you cannot operate the app — for example because you have already uninstalled it or cannot log in — please request deletion by email.

  • To: intaleslab@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Account deletion request"
  • Body: please include the email address registered to the account.

To prevent impersonation, we may verify that the request comes from you. After verifying your identity, we will carry out the deletion within 30 days of receipt as a guideline.

Data that will be deleted

Deleting your account removes the following data:

  • Account information (email address, nickname, user identifier (Firebase UID))
  • Character information (name, attribute, job, level, equipment) and inventory
  • In-game assets (various coupons, GP, level, etc.)
  • Friend information (including dissolving mutually registered friend relationships)
  • Store-visit sharing information (presence)
  • Authentication account (your account in Firebase Authentication)
  • Data stored on the device (encrypted credentials, visit history, various settings)
