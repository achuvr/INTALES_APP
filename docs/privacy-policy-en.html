Privacy Policy

1. Introduction (Scope of This Policy)

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter, this "Policy") sets forth the policy governing the handling of users' information in the mobile application "INTALES (intalescafe)" (hereinafter, the "App") provided by intalescafe (hereinafter, "we," "us," or "our").

The App is a QR-linked, mobile RPG-style application that lets users enjoy raising in-app characters, acquiring items, recording store visits, gacha, and more, by scanning QR codes posted at the store (cafe). It is distributed for iOS and Android.

This Policy applies to information that we acquire, use, and manage through use of the App. The App uses external services (Google/Firebase, Photon, etc.) to provide user authentication and various functions, and in this process some information about users is transmitted to external operators. We notify and publish, in this Policy, matters including those concerning such externally transmitted information. Users should review the contents of this Policy before using the App, and if they cannot agree to its contents, they should discontinue use of the App. Where the user's own consent is required under applicable law (such as provision to third parties located in foreign countries), we will separately obtain the necessary consent. For details on the handling of information by external services, the respective privacy policies of each company also apply (see Section 5).

Please note that the App currently does not have an explicit consent screen at first launch. We may revise the operation of this Policy in the future, such as by implementing a consent-acquisition flow.

3. Information We Acquire

The App acquires the following information to the extent necessary to provide the service. Note that the App does not acquire your name (legal name), date of birth/age, telephone number, address, payment/billing information, location information (GPS), or device-unique identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, etc.).

(a) Information that users enter or provide

  • Email address: Used for account registration and authentication (login). It is used as authentication information and is also stored as identifying information on the user profile.
  • Password: Used for authentication. Passwords are securely managed by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text in our database (see Section 13 below).
  • Nickname (username): Used for display within the App and for identification in the friend function.
  • Character information: Character name, attribute, job, level, and equipment contents (up to 6 slots per character).
  • In-game assets: Various coupons (5-piece coupon, 7-piece coupon, drink/coffee/attack coupons, etc.), GP, level, and other balances.
  • Friend information: The identifiers (UID) and display names of other users registered as friends, and the date and time of registration.
  • Store-visit sharing setting (presence sharing): The setting for whether or not to share visit status with friends (optional / opt-in).

(b) Information automatically acquired or generated by the App (account identifiers, etc.)

  • Firebase UID: A unique user identifier assigned to each account. Used for all communications involving authentication.
  • Timestamps such as registration date/time and last login date/time (lastDate, etc.).
  • Date information for bonus management (dates managing the acquisition status of visit bonuses, repeat-visit bonuses, and stay bonuses).
  • Inventory (owned items) reference information (combinations of job and item ID).
  • Visit-status timestamps (presence): Only when the "store-visit sharing setting" in (a) above is enabled, the visit time is recorded on an external server (Firestore's presence/store). If the sharing setting is not enabled, visit status is never transmitted to the server.
  • App instance identifier: An identifier issued by Firebase Installations to identify the app instance.
  • Identifiers derived from Google Play services, etc.: Due to the dependencies of the Google Play services libraries incorporated in the App, an advertising ID (AD_ID), install referrer information, attribution information, and the like may be transmitted to Google (see Sections 5 and 8). We do not use these for the purposes of ad delivery, ad measurement, or behavioral analysis.

Note that the visit records themselves (history of check-in/check-out times, stay duration, etc.) are, in principle, stored only on the device and are not transmitted to the server (see Section 10).

(c) Information acquired from the camera (reading QR codes)

The App uses the camera to read QR codes posted at the store. The camera footage is processed in real time within the device (in memory) solely for the purpose of extracting the string (text) recorded in the QR code. Captured images and video frames are not stored on the device, nor transmitted or uploaded externally. The extracted QR string itself is also not transmitted externally (only the results of the game processing carried out according to the QR contents are reflected to the server as necessary).

The main actions executed by QR codes are friend registration, membership card migration (paper → digital), gacha events, acquisition of buff cards, leveling up, coupon acquisition, unlocking new characters, and visit records (check-in/check-out).

4. Purposes of Use of Information

We use the acquired information within the scope of the following purposes.

  1. Authentication of users and account management (login and identity verification)
  2. Management and synchronization of character development, equipment, inventory, and in-game assets (coupons, GP, etc.)
  3. Provision of various functions based on QR code reading (item acquisition, leveling up, gacha, coupon granting, character unlocking, etc.)
  4. Provision of visit records and visit bonuses (sharing of visit status only when the user has enabled the sharing setting)
  5. Provision of the friend function and group cooperative play (multiplayer)
  6. Prevention of unauthorized use, and stable provision, maintenance, and improvement of the App
  7. Responding to inquiries from users
  8. Purposes incidental to the above purposes of use

9. Camera Access Permission

The App uses the camera access permission solely for the purpose of reading QR codes posted at the store.

  • Camera footage is processed within the device (in memory) solely for the purpose of analyzing the strings contained in QR codes.
  • Captured photos and video are not stored on the device, nor transmitted or uploaded to external servers.
  • On iOS, we display an explanation (purpose statement) regarding camera use, and use the camera with the user's permission. The relevant use is limited to reading QR codes. On Android, we request permission for the CAMERA permission.
  • Camera use permission can be changed (denied) at any time from each OS's settings. If permission is not granted, the QR code reading function cannot be used.

10. Information Stored Only Within the Device (Information Not Transmitted to the Server)

The following information is, in principle, stored only within the user's device and is not transmitted to our servers or external services.

  • Visit records (visit_log.json): History of check-in/check-out times, stay duration, etc.
  • Activity history (history_log.json): Operation history of dice, gacha, friends, visits, coupons, etc. (a circular buffer retaining up to 50 entries).
  • Various local caches: Cache of the item database, item icon images, and event image cache.
  • Encrypted authentication information (user.txt): Stored on the device encrypted with AES256.
  • Settings and progress (PlayerPrefs): Equipment state, win-streak records (battle streak), buff cards, attack bonuses, event image URL cache, etc.

Note that, as described above, in relation to visit records, the visit timestamp when "visit status sharing (presence)" is enabled, and progress data such as character levels accompanying visit bonuses, are transmitted to the server (Firestore). This is distinguished from the visit records themselves (history of times and stay durations), which are stored only within the device.

These on-device data can be erased by uninstalling the App or by using each OS's app data deletion function.

11. Location Information

The App does not acquire location information via GPS or the like. It does not request access permission for location services, nor does it use low-accuracy location information. Visit records are created based on the operation of reading the store's QR code, and do not measure or acquire the user's geographic location.

12. Data Retention Period

  • Account data on the server (the user profile, character information, in-game assets, friend information, etc. stored in Firestore) is retained while the user is using the account. If the user deletes their account, the relevant data is deleted (see Section 15).
  • Visit-status timestamps (presence): When the visit-status sharing setting is enabled, the latest state is overwritten and saved as needed.
  • Information stored within the device is retained on the device until the user uninstalls the App or deletes the app data. The activity history is capped at a maximum of 50 entries, and when the cap is exceeded, the oldest entries are automatically deleted in order.
  • Camera footage is not saved and is immediately discarded after the analysis of each frame.

The handling of accounts that have not been used for a long period may be set forth in this Policy in the future. Where there is information whose retention is required by law, we retain it for the period prescribed by the relevant law.

13. Security Management Measures

We take necessary and appropriate measures, including the following, to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of acquired information and otherwise for security management.

  • Authentication information (passwords) is managed by Firebase Authentication and is not stored in plain text in our database. Communications during authentication are encrypted.
  • Authentication information stored within the device is encrypted using AES256.
  • Communications with Google/Firebase are conducted over encrypted communication paths (HTTPS).
  • Appropriate access control is applied to data access involving authentication.

Note that, regarding the security management measures on the external services' side, they follow each company's privacy policy and security policy. For details of the technical measures on the external services' side, such as whether the communication path with Photon Cloud is encrypted, please review each company's published information.

15. Users' Rights (Disclosure, Correction, Suspension of Use, Deletion, etc.)

Users may request, with respect to their own retained personal data held by us, notification of the purpose of use, disclosure, correction/addition/deletion of contents, suspension/erasure of use, suspension of provision to third parties, and disclosure of third-party provision records.

  • Account deletion within the App: Using the App's account deletion function deletes the data on the server (visit sharing information (presence/store), friend information, character information, user profile document) and the data within the device (encrypted authentication information, visit records, activity history, various settings), and the account on Firebase Authentication is also deleted. Account deletion may require re-authentication for identity verification.
  • Information that may remain even after deletion: Due to the nature of the friend function, where the user's own UID and nickname are stored in a denormalized form on the account side of other users with whom they previously registered as friends, such information may remain on those other users' account side even after the user deletes their account. If you wish to have such residual information deleted, please contact the inquiry desk in Section 18.
  • Requests by inquiry: If you wish to request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, deletion, etc. by a method other than the above, or if you have any questions about the procedure, please contact the inquiry desk in Section 18.

When making a request for disclosure or the like, in order to confirm that the request is from the person themselves, we will conduct identity verification by the method prescribed by us. In principle, no fee is charged for responding to requests (if a fee is required for requests for notification of the purpose of use and disclosure of retained personal data, we will separately notify you of that fact). After confirming the contents of the request, we will respond within a reasonable period in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
